Quick answer

AutoKnowledge Pro is a free web-based vehicle diagnostic platform from Motor Vehicle Expert. It helps users organise vehicle symptoms, warning lights, fault-code direction, MOT risk and diagnostic reports in one place.

Use it when a vehicle has a warning light, no-start issue, overheating concern, braking fault, battery problem, MOT issue, strange smell, vibration, loss of power or repeated fault pattern.

Vehicle Health Score System

One of the strongest parts of AutoKnowledge Pro is the vehicle health score concept. Instead of looking at one warning light in isolation, it encourages users to think about the overall condition of the vehicle.

Healthy

No major warning signs are obvious, and the vehicle may only need normal monitoring or routine maintenance.

Check Soon

A warning light, symptom or system issue may need investigation before it becomes worse or more expensive.

Action Required

The vehicle may have a safety, reliability or MOT-related concern that should not be ignored.

Why this matters:

Drivers often focus on one symptom, such as a warning light or strange noise. A health score helps give a wider picture of the vehicle rather than treating each clue separately.

What AutoKnowledge Pro Cannot Do

No online diagnostic platform can fully inspect a vehicle through a screen. AutoKnowledge Pro can help organise symptoms, fault direction and MOT risk, but it cannot physically test parts or confirm every fault without real vehicle checks.

  • !It cannot replace a physical inspection.
  • !It cannot guarantee the exact failed component from one symptom.
  • !It cannot replace an MOT test or garage diagnostic scan.
  • !It should not be used to justify driving an unsafe vehicle.
  • !Serious symptoms should always be checked professionally.
Safe advice:

If the car has brake failure symptoms, severe overheating, oil pressure warnings, smoke, fuel smells, coolant loss or unsafe handling, stop safely and arrange professional help.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is AutoKnowledge Pro free?

Yes. AutoKnowledge Pro is free to launch through a web browser.

Is it only for drivers?

No. It can also support mobile mechanics, workshops and fleet users who want structured diagnostic sessions and report-style summaries.

Can it replace a mechanic?

No. It helps with fault direction, but it does not replace a physical inspection, MOT test or professional diagnosis.

Can it help with MOT risk?

Yes. It is built with MOT-aware guidance for warning lights, symptoms and vehicle faults that may affect an MOT.

Does it explain fault codes?

It can help with fault-code direction, but codes should be treated as clues rather than automatic parts replacement instructions.

Should I still get a garage check?

Yes, especially if the fault is safety-related, repeated, severe, or if the vehicle feels unsafe to drive.
