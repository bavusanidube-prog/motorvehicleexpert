Does MOT Check Service History UK?

An MOT does not check service history. The tester does not inspect service books, garage invoices, dealer stamps, digital service records or maintenance receipts as part of the MOT test.

A car can pass an MOT with no service history if it meets the required road safety and environmental standards on the day. However, missing service history can still be a major warning sign when buying a used car.

This guide explains the difference between MOT history and service history, whether missing records affect an MOT, what service history means for used car buyers and what checks to do before trusting a car with poor maintenance records.

Quick answer

Does an MOT check service history?

No. An MOT does not check service history. The tester does not require service records, dealer stamps, invoices or proof of maintenance to pass the vehicle.

The MOT is a roadworthiness and environmental inspection. It checks whether the car meets minimum legal standards on the day of the test.

Service history is separate. It helps show how well the car has been maintained over time, but it is not part of the MOT pass or fail decision.

Mechanic answer

No service history can still be a serious warning sign

A car can pass an MOT with no service history, but that does not mean it has been looked after properly.

Missing service history may hide skipped oil changes, old filters, overdue cambelt replacement, poor brake maintenance, coolant neglect or gearbox service issues.

So the MOT may say the car is legal today, while the missing service history tells you there may be future repair risk.

Service records

What Is Service History?

Service history is evidence that a vehicle has been maintained over time. It is not the same as MOT history.

Service stamps

Garage or dealer stamps

A stamped service book can show when routine servicing was carried out and by whom.

Invoices

Receipts and repair records

Invoices can prove oil changes, brake work, tyres, cambelt replacement, coolant work and other maintenance.

Digital records

Online service records

Some modern cars have digital service history stored by dealers or manufacturer systems.

Mileage proof

Mileage consistency

Service records help confirm whether mileage looks genuine alongside MOT history.

Maintenance

Proof of care

Regular servicing suggests the owner looked after the vehicle rather than only fixing MOT failures.

Buyer confidence

Used car value

Good service history can increase buyer confidence and help protect resale value.

MOT history

What MOT history tells you

MOT history shows test dates, mileage readings, failures, advisories and pass results. It is useful for spotting repeated defects, corrosion, tyre wear, brake issues and mileage inconsistencies.

But MOT history does not show whether the oil was changed, filters were replaced, cambelt was done or the car was serviced properly.

Service history

What service history tells you

Service history shows maintenance. It can reveal oil changes, filters, spark plugs, brake fluid, coolant, timing belt work, gearbox servicing and repairs.

A car with a clean MOT history but poor service history may still carry hidden engine, gearbox or long-term maintenance risk.

MOT result

Can A Car Pass MOT With No Service History?

Yes. Service history is not required for an MOT pass.

A vehicle can pass its MOT with no service book, no invoices and no proof of maintenance if it meets the required road safety and emissions standards on the day.

The MOT tester is not checking whether the car has had regular oil changes, whether the cambelt was replaced on time or whether the gearbox has been serviced.

But poor maintenance often leads to MOT problems over time. For example, neglected tyres, worn brakes, suspension wear, oil leaks, emissions faults and warning lights can all lead to advisories or failures.

Can pass

No service history

A car can pass if it is roadworthy and meets MOT standards on test day.

Can fail

Poor maintenance defects

If neglect has caused illegal tyres, worn brakes, leaks or emissions issues, the car can fail.

Buyer risk

Hidden repair costs

No service history can increase the risk of future engine, clutch, gearbox or cambelt costs.

Used car buying

Should You Buy A Car With No Service History But A Valid MOT?

A valid MOT is useful, but it should not be treated as proof the car has been well maintained.

Acceptable

If the car is cheap and inspected

A low-value older car with no history may still be worth considering if the condition, MOT history and inspection are strong.

Negotiate

If records are incomplete

Partial history should reduce confidence. Ask for proof of key jobs and budget for a full service.

Be cautious

If expensive jobs are unknown

Cambelt, gearbox, clutch, turbo, DPF and hybrid/EV maintenance gaps can become expensive.

Used car buyer warning

A seller may say “it has a full MOT” as if that proves the car is mechanically sound. It does not. An MOT is not a service, not a warranty and not a full used car inspection.

If there is no service history, check the MOT history carefully, inspect the car properly, test drive it, check warning lights, look for leaks and price future maintenance before buying.

Buyer checklist

What to check if service history is missing

  • Check MOT history for repeated advisories.
  • Compare mileage across MOT records.
  • Ask for invoices, not just stamps.
  • Look for cambelt or timing chain proof where relevant.
  • Check oil level and oil condition.
  • Inspect coolant condition and leaks.
  • Check brake, tyre and suspension condition.
  • Look for dashboard warning lights.
  • Test drive from cold if possible.
  • Budget for a full service immediately after purchase.
Best order

How to protect yourself before buying

If a car has no service history, do not rely only on the MOT. Use the MOT as one part of the decision.

  1. Check MOT history online.
  2. Inspect the vehicle condition.
  3. Ask for any repair invoices.
  4. Check whether major maintenance is due.
  5. Get a diagnostic scan if warning lights appear.
  6. Negotiate based on missing evidence.
  7. Walk away if the seller cannot explain gaps.

Frequently asked questions

Does MOT Check Service History FAQs

Clear answers to common UK driver questions about MOT history, service records and buying cars with missing history.

Does MOT check service history?

No. Service history is not checked during the MOT.

Can a car pass MOT with no service history?

Yes. If the car meets MOT standards on the day, missing service history does not automatically stop it passing.

Is MOT history the same as service history?

No. MOT history shows test results, mileage, failures and advisories. Service history shows maintenance and repairs.

Does service history affect MOT?

Not directly. But poor maintenance can create faults that fail the MOT.

Should I buy a car with no service history?

Only with caution. Check MOT history, condition, mileage, warning lights and repair risk before buying.

Can a valid MOT hide poor maintenance?

Yes. A valid MOT means the car met minimum standards at the time of the test, not that it has been serviced properly.
