Safe-To-Drive Decisions

When should you stop driving and seek help?

Not every fault needs immediate recovery, but some symptoms create a real safety or engine-damage risk. When deciding whether to continue, consider the affected system, how severe the symptom is and whether vehicle behaviour is changing.

Lower urgency

Monitor and arrange diagnosis

Comfort-system faults and minor non-safety issues can often be booked in without immediately stopping the vehicle.

Typical Risk: Lower
Medium urgency

Limit driving and diagnose soon

Amber warnings, developing vibration, minor clutch symptoms or charging concerns may allow limited use but should not be ignored.

Typical Risk: Medium
High urgency

Stop safely

Loss of braking, severe overheating, oil-pressure warnings, dangerous steering faults, major tyre damage, fuel leaks, heavy smoke or major loss of drive require immediate attention.

Typical Risk: High

Severe overheating High Head gasket or major engine damage. Stop safely and allow the system to cool.
