Quick Answer

What should you do if your MOT expiry date is wrong online?

First, check the official MOT history and confirm the date of your latest successful MOT test. Then compare the displayed expiry date with the government's early MOT renewal rules.

If the vehicle was tested more than one month minus one day before its previous MOT expired, the new expiry date may legitimately have moved forward. This is not necessarily a DVSA recording mistake.

If the expiry date still appears incorrect after checking the rules, use the official GOV.UK service for reporting a mistake on your MOT record. The service includes guidance specifically for reporting an incorrect MOT due date.

Example: an MOT expiry date that is not actually wrong

An MOT expiry date can appear incorrect for several reasons. Sometimes the online record needs investigating, but often the date has changed because the vehicle was tested outside the early renewal window.

Is an earlier MOT expiry date always a mistake?

No. If you have an MOT carried out too far in advance, you normally lose the ability to preserve the previous renewal date. The next expiry date is then based on the successful test date.

This can make the new MOT expiry appear earlier in the calendar than the date you previously expected. It does not mean the test centre or DVSA entered the wrong date.

Do not request a correction based on an assumption

Before reporting a wrong MOT expiry date, check whether the test was completed more than one month minus one day before the previous expiry. If it was, the date change may be correct.

UK MOT Renewal Rules

How early can you get an MOT without changing the expiry date?

GOV.UK states that you can have an MOT carried out up to one month minus one day before the existing expiry date and keep the same renewal date for the following year.

This rule is particularly important when investigating an MOT expiry date that appears incorrect. If the test took place inside the permitted renewal window, the existing anniversary date is normally retained.

If the test takes place earlier than that window, the new MOT expiry date is normally calculated from the date of the successful test.

A successful test after expiry normally creates a new expiry date based on the date of the new test.

Driving restrictions apply

Why does the one-month-minus-one-day rule matter?

The rule allows drivers to arrange an MOT before the current certificate expires without losing the existing annual renewal date.

For example, if your MOT expires on 15 May, the earliest date you can test and retain that renewal date is 16 April.

If the vehicle passes on 16 April, its new expiry date is 15 May the following year.

If it passes on 14 April, the new expiry date is 13 April the following year.

First MOT Rules

Can the first MOT due date be wrong online?

A vehicle's first MOT due date is different from the renewal date of a vehicle that already undergoes annual testing. For most ordinary cars in Great Britain, the first MOT is required by the third anniversary of registration.

However, some vehicle categories have different testing requirements. Imported vehicles, taxis and certain other vehicle classes may need additional consideration.

Can DVSA change an MOT expiry date?

DVSA can investigate reported MOT record errors and determine whether a correction is appropriate under the applicable rules.

What should you explain to DVSA?

Describe the issue in a clear chronological order. Explain when the previous MOT expired, when the latest successful test took place and what expiry date is currently displayed.

UK Driving Rules

Can you drive if the MOT expiry date is wrong online?

Whether you can legally drive depends on whether the vehicle has a valid MOT under the applicable rules, not simply on whether you disagree with the date displayed online.

If the official record shows that the MOT has expired, do not assume that an alleged recording error gives you permission to continue driving.

In general, a vehicle that requires an MOT must have a valid test certificate when used on public roads. Limited exceptions can apply, including journeys to a pre-arranged MOT test or certain repair appointments. The vehicle must still be roadworthy.

Valid MOT confirmed

If the vehicle has a valid MOT, normal legal requirements still apply, including tax, insurance and roadworthiness.

What if the MOT has expired while you wait for a correction?

If the recorded expiry date has passed and you cannot establish that the vehicle has a valid MOT, seek official clarification before driving.

Where necessary, arrange a new MOT rather than assuming an unresolved date dispute provides an exemption.

A vehicle must also remain roadworthy. A valid MOT does not make it legal to drive a vehicle with dangerous defects.

Important legal distinction

A genuinely incorrect online record and an MOT that has legally expired are not the same situation. If there is uncertainty, obtain clarification rather than relying on an assumed expiry date.

Frequently Asked Questions

Wrong MOT Expiry Date FAQs

These are common questions UK drivers ask about incorrect MOT expiry dates, early renewal rules, official record corrections and vehicle legality.

Why is my MOT expiry date wrong online?

Your MOT expiry date may appear wrong because the vehicle was tested earlier than the permitted renewal window, an old reminder contains outdated information or the official record needs investigating. Check the previous expiry date and latest successful test date first.

Can an incorrect MOT expiry date be corrected?

Yes, genuine MOT record errors can be reported through the official GOV.UK service. The relevant authority will determine whether a correction is appropriate. A date that changed correctly because of early testing is not normally an error.

How do I report a wrong MOT expiry date to DVSA?

Visit the GOV.UK service called Report a mistake on your MOT record and follow the instructions for an incorrect MOT due date. Prepare the vehicle registration, previous expiry date and latest successful MOT test details.

How early can I renew my MOT without changing the expiry date?

In Great Britain, you can normally renew an MOT up to one month minus one day before the existing expiry date and retain the same annual renewal anniversary.

Why did my MOT expiry date change after an early test?

If the vehicle passed its MOT earlier than the permitted renewal window, the new expiry date is normally calculated from the new successful test rather than the previous expiry anniversary.

Can the MOT garage change my expiry date?

The test centre may be able to explain the test details and renewal calculation, but you should not assume it can change an expiry date on request. Follow the official MOT record correction process for a suspected error.

What if my MOT certificate and online expiry date are different?

Check that both records refer to the same vehicle and test. A newer MOT may have changed the expiry date shown on an older certificate. If the discrepancy remains unexplained, follow the official GOV.UK correction guidance.

Can I drive while an MOT expiry date correction is pending?

A pending correction request does not automatically extend MOT validity. If the vehicle requires an MOT and you cannot establish that it has a valid certificate, do not assume ordinary road use is permitted. Seek official clarification.

What if my MOT has expired because the date is wrong?

Check the official MOT status and seek clarification through the appropriate government service. Do not assume that a disputed expiry date provides a legal exemption from MOT requirements. Limited driving exceptions may apply to qualifying journeys, such as a pre-arranged MOT test.

Can a first MOT due date be incorrect?

Yes, a first MOT due date may need investigating if the registration details or applicable vehicle category have been misunderstood. Most ordinary cars in Great Britain need their first MOT by the third anniversary of registration, but different rules apply to some vehicles.

Should I buy a car if the seller says the MOT date is wrong?

Check the official MOT status and history independently. Ask the seller to explain the discrepancy and provide relevant documentation. Do not rely on a claimed or pending correction as proof of a longer MOT validity period.

Where can I check my correct MOT expiry date?

Use the free official GOV.UK MOT status checker at www.gov.uk/check-mot-status. You can also review previous test dates using the GOV.UK MOT history service.

  • ✓ Recheck the online record.
  • ✓ Do not assume a pending correction extends validity.
Final legal reminder

If a vehicle legally requires an MOT, do not use it for ordinary journeys without a valid certificate. A disputed date or pending correction request does not automatically authorise driving. The vehicle must also remain roadworthy.
