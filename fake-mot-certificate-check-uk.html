Quick Answer

How do you check if an MOT certificate is genuine?

The most reliable way to check whether an MOT certificate is genuine is to verify the vehicle directly through the official GOV.UK MOT services. Enter the vehicle's registration number, check its current MOT status and review the recorded MOT history. Compare the test date, result, mileage and vehicle details with the certificate you have been shown.

For vehicles covered by the Great Britain MOT system, the official online record is a more reliable starting point than a seller's photograph, printed certificate or downloaded document. If the information does not match, do not immediately assume fraud: a recording mistake, registration change or missing record may also require investigation.

MOT Certificate Authenticity

What Is a Fake MOT Certificate?

A fake MOT certificate is a document that falsely represents a vehicle as having passed an MOT test, or misrepresents important details of a test. It may be entirely fabricated, altered from a genuine certificate or presented in a misleading way to make a vehicle appear to have a valid MOT.

Not every certificate discrepancy is fraud. Incorrect mileage, registration changes, administrative mistakes and missing MOT history entries can also create differences between paperwork and official records. The correct approach is to verify the evidence before deciding what happened.

Fabricated Certificate

A document may be created to resemble an official MOT certificate even though no corresponding test was carried out.

Serious authenticity concern

Altered Genuine Certificate

A genuine certificate may be changed to display a different result, expiry date, registration or other important information.

Verify original record

Genuine Certificate, Misleading Claim

A seller might present an authentic certificate from an earlier test as proof of current MOT validity.

Check current MOT status

Certificate Fraud and MOT Testing Fraud Are Different

An altered document and an improperly issued MOT result are not necessarily the same problem. A certificate could be forged without any corresponding electronic test record. Alternatively, a result might appear in the official database even though there are concerns about whether the test was performed properly.

Document Authenticity Problem

The paperwork does not accurately represent the official MOT record. The first step is to compare the document with GOV.UK MOT history.

Suspected Improper MOT Test

A test may have been recorded, but there is evidence suggesting the vehicle was not properly tested or the result was improperly issued.

Important distinction

An MOT result appearing in the official database does not prove that every inspection procedure was carried out correctly. If you have evidence of improper MOT testing, DVSA has a separate reporting procedure.

For official guidance on suspected MOT testing fraud, see GOV.UK: Report an MOT Tester.

MOT Database Explained

How Official MOT Records and Certificates Work

In Great Britain, MOT test results are recorded electronically. A vehicle that passes receives a recorded pass result, while a vehicle that fails has its failure recorded. Drivers can inspect available records using the official MOT history service.

A printed certificate is a document representing a test result. The electronic MOT record is the definitive official record for tests held in the Great Britain MOT database.

Official Electronic Record

Records the MOT test outcome, date, mileage and applicable defect information.

Primary verification source

Printed MOT Certificate

A paper copy of a test result that should be consistent with the official MOT record.

Compare with database

Digital Certificate

A downloadable certificate associated with a recorded MOT test.

Obtain through GOV.UK

Why a Paper Certificate Is Not Enough

A certificate may be genuine but outdated. It may also relate to a different vehicle, contain an administrative error or have been altered. That is why both current MOT status and historical test details should be checked.

Historical Test Result

Shows what was recorded at the time of a particular MOT. It does not guarantee the vehicle is currently roadworthy.

Current MOT Validity

Shows the current recorded MOT position for the vehicle. It should be checked separately from old paperwork.

Special case: contingency MOT certificates

Official contingency procedures may be used when the normal MOT testing system is unavailable. A legitimate contingency certificate may therefore need additional verification while the electronic record is being updated. Contact the issuing test centre or DVSA if the circumstances are unclear.

Official reference: GOV.UK MOT Testing Guide.

MOT Validity Explained

Fake MOT Certificate vs Expired MOT: What Is the Difference?

A fake MOT certificate and an expired MOT are different problems. A certificate may be completely genuine but no longer provide evidence of current MOT validity. Conversely, a fabricated document may claim that a vehicle has a valid MOT when no matching test is recorded.

Genuine, Currently Valid MOT

The official MOT status confirms a current certificate. The vehicle must still be roadworthy.

Verify normal legal requirements

Genuine but Expired MOT

The historical test was genuine, but its validity period has ended. Ordinary road use may be unlawful if an MOT is required.

Check before driving

Suspected Fake Certificate

The document contains significant inconsistencies or cannot be reconciled with official records.

Investigate authenticity

MOT Test Centre Verification

Can an MOT Garage Confirm Whether a Certificate Is Genuine?

If the certificate identifies an MOT test centre, contacting the garage can help establish whether the claimed test took place. The centre may be able to clarify the test details or explain the appropriate official reporting process.

However, a garage's verbal assurance should not replace independent verification of the official MOT history and current status.

Check the certificate for relevant test centre information and confirm the business independently.

3. Ask for Clarification

Ask whether the test was carried out and whether the recorded result agrees with the supplied document.

4. Follow Official Procedures

If the discrepancy remains unresolved, use the appropriate DVSA or GOV.UK guidance.

UK MOT Driving Rules

Can You Drive a Car If Its MOT Certificate Might Be Fake?

Whether you can legally drive depends on the vehicle's actual MOT status, whether an MOT is required and whether the vehicle is roadworthy. A suspicious certificate does not by itself establish that the vehicle has no valid MOT. Equally, possessing a printed certificate does not establish that it currently has one.

Check the official MOT status before using the vehicle. If the required MOT cannot be confirmed, avoid ordinary road use until the position has been clarified.

Valid MOT Confirmed

The official MOT status shows current validity. The vehicle must still meet all other legal and roadworthiness requirements.

Check vehicle condition

No Valid MOT Confirmed

If the vehicle requires an MOT and does not have a valid one, ordinary road use is generally not permitted.

Do not use normally

Dangerous Vehicle Defect

If the vehicle has a dangerous defect, do not drive it until the defect has been repaired.

Immediate safety concern

What About Driving to an MOT Test?

Limited legal exceptions may permit a vehicle without a current MOT to be driven to a pre-arranged MOT test or in certain circumstances for repairs. These exceptions do not remove the requirement for the vehicle to be roadworthy.

A vehicle with a dangerous defect should not be driven on the road. Arrange suitable recovery or transport instead.

Frequently Asked Questions

Fake MOT Certificate Check UK FAQs

These are common questions UK drivers and used-car buyers ask about genuine MOT certificates, online verification, suspicious paperwork, incorrect records and legal driving requirements.

How can I check if an MOT certificate is genuine?

Use the official GOV.UK MOT history service to find the recorded test. Compare the registration number, test date, result and mileage with the certificate. Check the current MOT status separately to confirm whether the vehicle has a valid MOT.

Can someone fake an MOT certificate?

Yes. A document can be fabricated or altered to resemble an official MOT certificate. However, unusual formatting or a missing entry is not conclusive proof of fraud. Always compare the document with official records.

Is a printed MOT certificate proof of a valid MOT?

No. A printed certificate may relate to a genuine previous test but could have expired. Check the official MOT status to establish current validity. A certificate also does not guarantee that the vehicle is roadworthy.

Can I check an MOT certificate online for free?

Yes. The official GOV.UK MOT history and MOT status services are free. You can check recorded tests and current validity using the vehicle's registration number.

What if the MOT certificate does not appear in the history?

Check the registration, test date and available historical coverage. If a recent completed test is missing, contact the MOT test centre and ask it to verify the recorded result. Follow official guidance if the discrepancy remains unresolved.

Can a fake MOT certificate show a genuine registration number?

Yes. A registration number can be copied onto misleading paperwork. Checking the registration alone is not enough. Compare the claimed test with official history and verify the vehicle's identity separately.

How do I check an MOT certificate PDF?

Do not rely on the PDF's appearance alone. Open the GOV.UK MOT history service independently, locate the relevant test and compare its details. Where available, download the official certificate for comparison.

Does a wrong mileage reading mean the MOT certificate is fake?

Not necessarily. A genuine MOT test can contain an incorrect mileage entry. Compare the recorded mileage with previous MOT results, service records and supporting evidence. Use the official correction process where appropriate.

Can an MOT certificate have the wrong expiry date?

Yes. An apparent discrepancy may result from misunderstanding MOT renewal rules, looking at an older certificate or a genuine record error. Compare the full expiry date with the official MOT status and test history.

Can a number plate change affect MOT certificate checks?

Yes. A vehicle may have carried a different registration when an earlier MOT was completed. Compare the vehicle's VIN, V5C and registration history if older paperwork shows a different number plate.

Can an MOT garage confirm whether a test was carried out?

The test centre may be able to confirm relevant details of a test it performed and investigate a discrepancy. Contact the garage using independently obtained contact details. Continue to verify the official MOT record.

Who should I report a suspected fake MOT certificate to?

The appropriate reporting route depends on the concern. Suspected improper MOT testing can be reported through DVSA guidance. Suspected criminal fraud may require police or fraud reporting, while incorrect records should follow the relevant MOT correction procedure.

Can I drive if my MOT certificate might be fake?

Check the vehicle's actual MOT status and whether an MOT is legally required. If required validity cannot be established, avoid ordinary road use until the position is clarified. Never drive a vehicle with a known dangerous defect.

Does a valid MOT prove a used car is safe to buy?

No. An MOT pass records the outcome of an inspection at a particular time. It is not a full mechanical inspection, service history, ownership check or guarantee against future repairs. Inspect the vehicle and its history before buying.

What should I do if a seller refuses an MOT history check?

Do not rush into the purchase. Independently verify the MOT history and vehicle identity. If the seller refuses reasonable checks or cannot explain significant discrepancies, consider walking away.

Is a missing MOT record always evidence of fraud?

No. A missing record can result from an incorrect registration, historical coverage limits, a recording problem or other circumstances requiring investigation. Confirm the details with the test centre and follow official guidance before drawing conclusions.

Final Thoughts

Verify the MOT Record Before Trusting the Certificate

A genuine MOT certificate is useful evidence of a recorded test, but it should never be treated as the only proof of current MOT validity. The official GOV.UK MOT history and status services provide the starting point for checking whether the claimed test exists and whether the vehicle currently has a valid MOT.

If the certificate and official record do not agree, investigate the discrepancy before assuming fraud. Incorrect mileage, older registrations, historical records and administrative mistakes can all require different verification or correction procedures.

When buying a used car, combine MOT verification with vehicle identity checks, service history, ownership paperwork, mechanical inspection and an independent vehicle history check. A valid MOT does not guarantee that a vehicle is free from faults or safe throughout the certificate period.
